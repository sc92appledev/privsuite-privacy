Privacy Policy — PRIV-Suite

Privacy by design. Transparency. User control.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy describes how PRIV-Suite handles privacy, support requests and visits to this website. PRIV-Suite is a single application with the functions listed below.

1. Data controller

For personal-data processing whose purposes and means I determine in connection with PRIV-Suite, the data controller is Simone Cecere. For privacy or personal-data requests, contact: sc92.appledev@pm.me

2. App functions

  • Calculator: Basic arithmetic, percentages and information about rounded results and repeating decimals.
  • Time calculator: Addition and subtraction of durations, with conversions from milliseconds to millennia using the stated fixed time conventions.
  • Time span: Time elapsed between two dates and times, accounting for calendar months, leap years and daylight saving time in the device’s time zone.
  • Age calculator: Age calculation from a date of birth, with optional birth time and optional continuous updates.
  • Discount: Discounted prices, savings and totals for multiple prices, without a preset currency.
  • Converter: Length, mass, speed, area, volume, temperature, pressure, energy, power and digital data, with addition and subtraction of compatible quantities. Temperatures have separate value and difference modes.
  • Fuel consumption: Actual and average fuel consumption, fuel needed for a distance, driving range and optional costs, with metric, US and UK units.
  • Random generator: Letters, numbers, symbols, emoji or a mixture, with one to three elements and optional accented letters and extra symbols.

Also included are unit price comparison for mass, volume or items and direct and inverse proportions. All processing remains local.

3. Local processing and system clipboard

Calculations and random generation take place locally on the device. The developer does not collect or transmit input values, results or generation preferences to remote servers. These functions do not require an Internet connection or an account. PRIV-Suite does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Input values, dates of birth, times, settings and results are kept only in memory during the current app session. They remain available when switching functions and are discarded when the app terminates; they are not saved for future sessions. Data explicitly copied to the system clipboard follows the operating system’s behavior and settings.

The Copy function uses the system clipboard only after an explicit user action. PRIV-Suite does not automatically read clipboard contents. Copied data is also subject to the operating system's behavior and settings.

The calculator reads clipboard contents only when you explicitly use Paste. Single numeric results copied from PRIV-Suite also include a numeric representation so the calculator can paste the value without its unit. External text is accepted only as a supported number; ambiguous separators require your choice. Clipboard contents are not read automatically or transmitted.

4. Support email

If you email support, I use the information in your message to review and manage your request, reply and provide assistance. Please do not include unnecessary personal information. Messages are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and, where applicable, for legal or rights-protection purposes.

The email provider processes technical and other data associated with its service according to its own privacy notice and its role in each processing activity. It may process data for its own purposes, separately from my handling of support requests.

5. Website hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. To provide and secure its services, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses, device information, request data and access logs under its own privacy notice and according to its role in each processing activity. Reading this website is separate from using PRIV-Suite offline.

GitHub General Privacy Statement

6. Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, personal data is processed on the legal basis appropriate to the circumstances, for example to respond to a person's request or comply with legal obligations. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, object to processing or exercise data portability rights where applicable. For processing for which I am responsible, contact the email address above.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Italy: Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante).

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes or new features within PRIV-Suite, services used or applicable obligations. New features will be integrated into the same app. The date above indicates the latest update.
