Privacy Policy — PrivTools

Privacy by design. Transparency. User control.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy describes the privacy practices of the PrivTools applications listed below, including PRIV-Suite, as well as support requests and visits to this website. Listing an application does not imply that it is currently available on the App Store.

1. Data controller

For processing whose purposes and means I determine in connection with these applications, the data controller is Simone Cecere. For privacy or personal-data requests, contact: sc92.appledev@pm.me.

2. Applications covered

PRIV-Suite

PRIV-Suite combines a numerical calculator, a time calculator and a random generator for letters, numbers, symbols and emoji, with optional accented letters and extra symbols.

Priv-TimeCalc

Priv-TimeCalc performs addition, subtraction and conversion of durations.

Priv-Calc

Priv-Calc performs basic mathematical operations.

Priv-Tris

Priv-Tris is a tic-tac-toe game for two players on the same device.

Priv-Random

Priv-Random generates random letters, numbers, symbols and emoji.

3. Local processing and system clipboard

Calculations, random generation and gameplay take place locally on the device. The developer does not collect or transmit input values, results, game moves or generation preferences to remote servers. These functions do not require an Internet connection or an account. The applications do not intentionally include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking tools.

In applications offering Copy, the system clipboard is used only after an explicit user action. The applications do not automatically read clipboard contents. Copied data is also subject to the operating system's behavior and settings.

4. Future applications

New applications may be added to PrivTools. Their features and data practices may differ. This policy will be updated where necessary, and additional information may be provided for individual applications.

5. Support email

If you email support, I use the information in your message to review and manage your request, reply and provide assistance. Please do not include unnecessary personal information. Messages are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and, where applicable, for legal or rights-protection purposes.

The email provider processes technical and other data associated with its service according to its own privacy notice and its role in each processing activity. It may process data for its own purposes, separately from my handling of support requests.

6. Website hosting

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. To provide and secure its services, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses, device information, request data and access logs under its own privacy notice and according to its role in each processing activity. Reading this website is separate from using the applications offline.

GitHub General Privacy Statement

7. Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR applies, personal data is processed on the legal basis appropriate to the circumstances, for example to respond to a person's request or comply with legal obligations. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, object to processing or exercise data portability rights where applicable. For processing for which I am responsible, contact the email address above.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Italy: Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante) .

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the applications, services used or applicable obligations. The date above indicates the latest update.
